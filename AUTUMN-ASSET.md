# Autumn foliage asset

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool using fall-reference.png as a style reference.

Prompt: Create a standalone web decoration asset based on ONLY the detailed autumn vine border in the reference screenshot. Output a single long horizontal naturalistic autumn garland on genuinely transparent background, wide 3:1 canvas. NO website, NO text, NO photo, NO rectangular border. One gracefully winding thin woody copper vine runs left to right across the middle of the canvas, with elegant curling golden tendrils and irregular clusters of highly detailed maple and oak leaves, amber, deep orange, burnt sienna, burgundy, and a few shaded brown acorns. Finely serrated realistic leaf edges, visible veins, dimensional painted botanical shading, rich warm highlights and soft shadows exactly like the reference's foliage border. Leaves vary in size, orientation and spacing, not repetitive. Garland itself is thin and graceful occupying middle quarter of canvas height with transparent space above and below; branches reach and touch both left and right image edges. Premium detailed botanical illustration, not flat icons or clipart. This will be repeated along website edges and animated.

Source: public/autumn/garland-source.png. Optimized alpha-preserving runtime asset: public/autumn/garland.webp.

The final garland is attached to the branded sticky header, with seven drifting leaves and no side or bottom borders. Clicking or activating the vine button by keyboard releases twelve temporary leaves; the burst is capped at sixty elements and cleaned up after animation. Reduced motion disables movement and leaf bursts. Only the vine button receives pointer input; falling leaves do not intercept page controls.
